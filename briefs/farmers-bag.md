# Brief: Farmers Bag (shopper side)

Read AGENT_BRIEF.md first: its tools, rules and final report all apply.
Only the goal and the account details change.

Farmers Bag lets a shopper order from several local farms in one cart and pick the
order up at a farmers market the farms already attend. The directory covers the
whole United States.

- Your goal instead of the persona's, as your persona: find a farmers market, look at
  two different farms, put products from both farms in one cart, and get as far
  toward placing the order as the app lets you. If the app says ordering is not open
  yet, that is expected: report how clearly it says so.
- If nothing is near your phone's location, search or pick a US city (for example
  Austin, TX or Portland, OR) and carry on.
- Account: sign up with `<persona>+clerk_test@example.com` (your persona's name in
  lower case). The email code for that address is `424242`.
- Never enter card or payment details: stop at the payment step and report it.
- Out of scope: selling (the "Sell" or farmer sign-up flow) and deleting the account.
- Also look at: the cart with items from two farms, a farm's page, a market's page,
  and the You tab.
